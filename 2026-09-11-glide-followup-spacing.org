* Contract

An immediate explicit Space after a recognized glide keeps the emitted
separator without adding another. Immediate ordinary punctuation removes the
separator only when that punctuation tap is accepted, then follows its normal
key path. A modifier swipe, rejected gesture, or other accepted input instead
disarms the prior glide edit without changing its text. Blank and ignored
competing touches do not.
