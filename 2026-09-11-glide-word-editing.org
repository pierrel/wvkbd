#+title: Glide word-space editing amendment
#+date: 2026-09-11

* Historical supersessions

This amendment follows =2026-09-10-geometry-glide-recognizer.org=. Recognition
and geometry remain unchanged; this records the post-recognition editing unit.

* Contract

A recognized ASCII glide emits its word and one balanced Space and retains only
the bounded word-length-plus-one count. The next accepted plain Backspace emits
that many balanced Backspaces and clears the count first. Any other accepted
key or gesture clears it. Blank and ignored competing touches do not. No timer,
persistence, application integration, or external cursor tracking is added.
