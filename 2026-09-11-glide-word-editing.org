* Historical supersessions

This supersedes only the no-automatic-Space/retraction parts of

unknown words= and =Explicit non-goals=. Recognition and geometry remain
unchanged.

* Contract

A recognized ASCII glide emits up to 24 letters and one balanced Space and
retains only the bounded word-length-plus-one count. The next accepted plain
Backspace is the one-shot retraction: it emits that many balanced Backspaces
and clears the count first. Any other accepted key or gesture clears it. Blank
and ignored competing touches do not. No timer, persistence, application
integration, or external cursor tracking is added. Known lifecycle and
layout-context resets also disarm it. A later recognized glide replaces that
state with its own word-space unit.
