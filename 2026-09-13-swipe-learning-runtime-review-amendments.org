* Accepted changes

The capture-active no-candidate surface has exactly two full-width cells:
=Misswipe= and =Missing=.  An ordinary input outside the strip clears the
surface without inventing attribution.  Resolution rejects candidate versus
no-candidate outcome mismatches without consuming the pending identity, and
only a candidate gesture can become a correction.  Exact-unit Backspace keeps
that correction pending through Shift and resolves it on the next text key or
reswipe.  The final schema-valid gesture identifier is emitted; the following
allocation attempt closes observation instead of wrapping to identifier zero.
Output removal and fractional-scale lifecycle resets clear pending learning
identity.

The dictionary digest is exposed once by =glide.c=; =glide-learning.c= no
longer compiles a second copy of the generated dictionary merely to read its
digest.  The internal fd3 option is present in CLI help and remains exact.

send-failure fail-off, maximum encoding, candidate/no-candidate outcome
compatibility, overflow fail-off, two-cell right-edge attribution,
outside-strip unresolved behavior, and Backspace then manual correction.  The
existing pointer/touch suggestion, modifier, lifecycle, native, sanitizer,
