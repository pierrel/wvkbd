#+title: Native Hide tab exclusive-zone positioning correction
#+date: 2026-09-15

* Historical supersessions

This supersedes the positioning mechanism in
=2026-09-15-hide-tab-logical-position.org=, section =Correction=.  The
keyboard's layer surface already reserves its requested logical height as an
exclusive zone.  A second bottom margin on the Hide surface applies that
height again, leaving a full keyboard-height gap.

* Correction

The expanded Hide surface uses a zero bottom margin and follows the
compositor's usable bottom edge above the keyboard's exclusive zone.  When the
keyboard is collapsed, destroying its layer surface removes that exclusive
zone, so the same zero-margin control naturally moves to the physical bottom.
This also lets the compositor reposition the control when output scale or
orientation changes without a duplicate client-side height calculation.

The client no longer sends or recalculates any explicit margin.  Live
acceptance uses a compositor screenshot because a single-surface protocol mock
cannot model interaction between two layer surfaces' exclusive zones.
